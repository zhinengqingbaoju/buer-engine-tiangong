Decision: decision_id,category,content,status(proposed/discussed/approved/superseded),approved_by,approved_at,replaces_decision_id,impact_scope,source
Asset: asset_id,name,category,base_identity_id,state_version,canonical_descriptor,actual_media_refs,reference_roles,do_not_inherit,source_decisions,status(planned/candidate/testing/approved/locked/deprecated),test_scope,passed_conditions,untested_conditions,known_defects,drive_urls
Scene: scene_id,script_version,location_asset_version,character_states,prop_states,GEO_version,continuity_in,continuity_out,scene_status,open_issues
GEO: geo_id,scene_id,version,landmarks,entrances,exits,spatial_relations,screen_directions,axis_180,main_light_direction,restricted_zones,notes
Shot: shot_id,scene_id,narrative_function,contract_version,duration_target,opening_state,closing_state,active_characters,asset_versions,geo_version,camera_contract,action_beats,dialogue_verbatim,audio_requirements,continuity_in,continuity_out,reference_needs,risk_focus,status
Prompt: prompt_id,object_id,version,target_model,adapter_version,parent_prompt_version,actual_text,reference_mapping,platform_settings,changed_variables,change_reason,source_versions,unsupported_requirements,assumptions,status
Generation: generation_id,object_id,prompt_id,prompt_version,model,model_version,adapter_version,actual_references,actual_parameters,send_status,provider_status,result_file,drive_url,reroll_index,cost_evidence,qa_status,selection_status
Issue: issue_id,object_id,generation_id,category,severity,expected,observed,evidence_location,responsibility_layer,root_cause_status,recommended_route,status,resolved_by_version
Repair: repair_id,issue_id,route,changed_variables,preserve_requirements,source_version,new_version,expected_improvement,retest_result,regression_result,decision
Selection: selection_id,object_id,generation_id,status(candidate/rejected/adopted/locked/replaced),user_confirmation,known_defects,downstream_usage,replaces_selection_id
Task: task_id,project_id,target_object,stage,target_role,goal,required_inputs,preserve_requirements,permissions,status,result_refs
Edit: edit_id,edit_version,timeline,tracks,shot_generation_map,in_out,audio_usage,markers,transitions,cleanup_status,color_status,subtitle_status,open_gaps,final_export,review_status
Source: source_id,category,authority_level,title,version_date,hash_url,current_status,applicable_modules,notes
RouteChange: change_id,problem_found,old_route,new_route,reason,evidence,impact_scope,rollback,result

Prompt只引用事实；Generation绑定Prompt；Issue绑定实际Generation；Repair绑定Issue；Selection绑定Generation；Edit绑定采用结果。上游改变使下游当前资格失效，但历史保留。
